## PR #110 — Phase 8 Roadmap

- **Summary:** Authored `docs/roadmap/phase08-roadmap.md` in PR #110 (`cc3eede`, `2026-11-15`) breaking Phase 8 (UI/UX redesign and visual dogfooding, inserted by PR #108 on `2026-11-13` ahead of Phase 9 Release 2.0) into ten executable steps (`Step 1` through `Step 10`) with 60 static checks and the 38-row V1–V10 post-implementation verification matrix (`98` total checks in `--post`), bumped `opentelemetry-api` to `1.45.2` (`CVE-2026-126360`), refreshed the `roadmodel` planning kit to `0.2.114`, and added `v2.0.0-phase-8` to `docs/roadmap/ROADMAP.md`'s "Release & tagging" table.
- **Findings & judgement calls:**
  - **Engine vocabulary drift in the Phase 5–7 UI:** Auditing the frontend against `src/weatherbot/models/ladder.py`, `src/weatherbot/engine/rules.py`, and `src/weatherbot/engine/official.py` revealed that `ThreatLevelBadge`'s fallback names (`"Hurricane Warning / Evacuation"`, `"Tropical Storm Warning / Hurricane Watch"`, `"Storm Watch"`, `"Awareness"`) disagreed with `AlertLevel`'s official labels, and `Settings.tsx`'s hand-written `LADDER_RULES` listed rules in the wrong evaluation order, under a nonexistent rule ID (`tropical_storm_cone` instead of `storm_cone`), and credited products and codes (`Extreme Wind Warning`, `Storm Surge Warning`, `Storm Surge Watch`, `50-kt` bands, `TRW`, `TRA`) that the engine does not read. Decided to add `GET /api/ladder` (Decision 8) so the server is the single source of truth for the threat vocabulary.
  - **Synthetic Atlantic storm in live feed formats (Decision 2):** Because `AssessmentService` refuses replay snapshots and archive `D_Sphere` geometry must never be relabelled as WGS 84, decided to stage a synthetic Atlantic storm (`AL802026`, slot `AT1`) in the live feeds' own formats at two decimal places, labelled synthetic in its own fields while keeping `Provenance.source_id` on the closed `SourceId` register literals.
  - **Zero inline `style=` vs. Radix `Tabs` and `Collapsible` (Decisions 5, 7, 13):** Radix `Tabs` and `Collapsible` inject inline `style` attributes on mount, which fail the strict CSP (`style-src 'self'` with per-response nonce, no `unsafe-inline`) and eight Vitest `[style]` assertions. Decided to use native `<details>`/`<summary>` (`Disclosure`) for progressive disclosure, a custom WAI-ARIA `Tabs` primitive for `/settings`, and a portalled Radix `Popover` for `Export report`.
- **Disposition:** Merged in PR #110 (`cc3eede`, `2026-11-15`).
